# Missions

## Planned

1. **The Cipher Machine** — A Soviet signals unit has deployed a next-gen encryption device to a mountain outpost. Jacob must photograph the internals and get out before the cipher officer returns from dinner.

2. **Defector** — A Soviet rocketry scientist wants to come West. He's being held at a safe house by his own side pending "re-evaluation." Extract him alive, or recover the documents he smuggled out if he can't be saved.

3. **The Nuclear Train** — A decommissioned warhead is being transported by rail across Eastern Europe for "disposal." It's going somewhere it shouldn't. Jacob boards at one station and has until the next to find it, photograph the manifest, and get off without the GRU escort noticing.

4. **Blacklist** — The identities of every NATO asset in the Balkans are on a single piece of microfilm, currently in a Soviet rezidentura attached to their embassy. Steal it or destroy it before the morning diplomatic pouch.

5. **The Vanishing Colonel** — A British colonel on a skiing holiday in Austria has missed three check-ins. His chalet is now occupied by people who are definitely not skiers. Find out what they took from him and whether he's still useful to anyone.

6. **Red Mercury** — A rogue arms dealer is brokering the sale of fissile material to a buyer M won't name over the radio. The exchange is tonight at a casino. Jacob has one evening to identify both parties and stop the handoff without starting a diplomatic incident.

7. **Scorched Earth** — A Soviet listening post disguised as a weather station sits on neutral territory. It's been monitoring NATO naval movements for two years. HQ wants it destroyed. The station chief suspects something is coming — he's doubled the guard.

8. **The Sleeper** — A senior civil servant in Whitehall is a long-dormant Soviet asset who's just been activated. Jacob has one night in the man's country estate to find his dead-drop materials and plant disinformation before the asset completes his first act.

9. **Broken Arrow** — A US tactical nuclear shell has gone missing from a West German NATO depot. The Americans don't know yet. The Soviets might. Jacob has to find it first and ensure it quietly disappears — without either superpower knowing the other knows.

10. **The Dossier** — A retired KGB general is dying in a Swiss clinic and wants to sell his memoirs — forty years of operations, names, methods. Three services are sending someone tonight. Jacob needs the manuscript and needs to ensure the other two leave empty-handed.
